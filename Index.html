SIP BASICS

Learn the basics

A few simple points to understand before investing.

01

What is a SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) lets you invest a fixed amount at regular intervals, often monthly, in a mutual fund.

02

What is compounding?

Compounding means potential returns can earn further returns over time. The actual result depends on investment performance.

03

Is a SIP guaranteed?

No. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risk. Returns can vary, and you may receive less than you invested.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change my monthly SIP amount?

Many mutual fund plans allow you to modify, pause, or stop future instalments, subject to the fund's rules.

Does this calculator predict exact returns?

No. It uses the return rate entered by you to calculate an illustration. Actual market returns are not fixed.

Is this website giving investment advice?

No. It is an educational project and calculator. Consider your own circumstances and consult a qualified professional if needed.
